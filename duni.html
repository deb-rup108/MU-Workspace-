The concept of premium products has evolved from a simple marker of high price to a complex psychological and economic phenomenon. In modern commerce, a premium product is defined not just by its cost, but by its ability to offer superior quality, exclusive design, and emotional value that justifies a higher price point. As consumer preferences shift from material possession to experiential value, understanding the dynamics of premiumization becomes essential for businesses trying to survive in a competitive marketplace.
